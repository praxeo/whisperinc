How to record
  • One line is one take. Hold Ctrl+Space, read it at the pace you normally dictate, release. The page catches the paste, shows a check, and moves on.
  • This page does not show what was transcribed, on purpose: seeing it would tempt you to read again until it comes out right, and the test needs the plain first attempt. Read again only when you actually stumbled.
  • If you stumble, use Read it again. The last attempt of a line is the one that counts.
  • If the paste never arrives (a "Copied, paste it" flash, or a take with no words), press Next yourself. Arrow keys work too: right for next, left for back, R to read again, S to skip.
  • Read units the way you say them: mg is milligrams, 2.4 ATA is "two point four A-T-A", 96 over 58 is "ninety-six over fifty-eight".
  • Record only when no patient conversation is audible in the background.
  • When you are done, or want to stop, press Copy session log and paste it to Claude. Progress is kept in this browser.
